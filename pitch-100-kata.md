# Pitch — 100 Kata (Dispute Compass)

PayPal disputes run on strict deadlines with formal evidence rules. Most users lose not because they’re wrong—but because they miss a 10-day window or don’t know what evidence counts. Once closed, a dispute cannot be reopened.

Dispute Compass is an AI Case Coach for PayPal disputes. It guides buyers and sellers through triage, a deterministic deadline engine, guided evidence collection, a strength meter, settlement advice, and a formal appeal letter—submitted via PayPal Disputes API Sandbox. Output: a letter ready for PayPal Resolution Center, with verified policy quotes and an Integrity Guard against false claims.

Know where you stand. Settle smart. Escalate right.
