# Script Video Demo — 90 Detik (Dispute Compass)

**Durasi target:** ≤ 90 detik  
**Bahasa:** English (demo) / Indonesian (narasi optional)  
**Karakter:** Sari (penjual kecil) dan Budi (pembeli) — cerita singkat

---

## [0:00–0:10] Masalah

**Visual:** Layar PayPal Resolution Center. Notifikasi claim muncul. Timer merah berkedip.

**Narasi (EN):**  
“Sari sells handmade gear online. A buyer files an Item Not Received claim. She has 10 days to respond. She doesn’t know what evidence counts. The clock is ticking.”

**Tampilan teks:** “10 hari untuk merespons. Bukti apa yang diperlukan?”

---

## [0:10–0:25] Masuk ke Dispute Compass

**Visual:** Browser buka Dispute Compass. Form sederhana: pilih peran (Penjual), jenis (Item Not Received), detail transaksi.

**Narasi:**  
“Dispute Compass is an AI Case Coach for PayPal disputes. Pick your role, your dispute type, and your transaction details.”

**Aksi:** Klik “Generate Response”.

---

## [0:25–0:40] Triage & Deadline Engine

**Visual:** Halaman hasil triage. Progress bar. Countdown timer. Langkah berikutnya ditampilkan.

**Narasi:**  
“Triage shows where you are, how much time is left, and the next step. Deterministic deadline engine—no guesswork.”

**Tampilan teks:** “Stage: Claim. Response due in: 7 days. Next: Submit evidence.”

---

## [0:40–0:60] Case Coach & Strength Meter

**Visual:** Pengumpulan bukti terpandu (tracking number, chat screenshot). Strength Meter berubah dari Weak → Fair. Resolution Advisor menampilkan 3 opsi: Settle / Fight Fair / Escalate Right.

**Narasi:**  
“Case Coach collects evidence, rates your case strength, and recommends: Settle, Fight Fair, or Escalate Right. Sari chooses Fight Fair.”

**Tampilan teks:** “Strength: Weak → Fair. Recommendation: Fight Fair with evidence pack.”

---

## [0:60–0:75] Evidence Pack & Letter (PayPal API Sandbox)

**Visual:** Surat appeal formal di-generate. Tombol “Send via PayPal API (Sandbox)”. Status berubah di sandbox.

**Narasi:**  
“Evidence Pack generates a formal appeal letter via NVIDIA NIM, with static fallback. Submits through PayPal Disputes API Sandbox—OAuth, provide-evidence, appeal.”

**Tampilan teks:** “Letter generated. Submitted via PayPal Disputes API Sandbox.”

---

## [0:75–0:90] Penutup

**Visual:** T&C Navigator menampilkan kutipan klausul PayPal dengan versi dan tanggal. Logo Dispute Compass. Tagline.

**Narasi:**  
“T&C Navigator shows exact PayPal policy quotes—never hallucinated. Know where you stand. Settle smart. Escalate right. Dispute Compass. PayPal AI Hackathon 2026.”

**Tampilan teks:** “Dispute Compass — Know where you stand. Settle smart. Escalate right.”

---

## Catatan Produksi

- Suara narasi: kalimat pendek, nada rekan kerja (bukan ceramah)
- Overlay teks: maksimum 8 kata per layar
- Musik latar: ringan, non-mengganggu
- Akhir: call-to-action ke repo GitHub + Devpost submission link
